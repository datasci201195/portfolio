This sample poses a question a rider might ask. If two buses arrive together, how long does the next passenger wait?

A small analysis could group arrivals by stop and direction, plot the gaps between consecutive buses, and compare those gaps with the timetable. The choice of stop, time window, and missing observations would belong in the project notes.
